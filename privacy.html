Waitlist privacy notice

Last updated: 26 September 2026

The short version: if you email us to join the waitlist, we keep your email address and message to tell you when Permwise launches, and about testing if you asked. We don't share it for marketing, and you can ask us to remove it any time.

Who we are

Permwise is made by Takudzwa Mugwamba, who is responsible for your information. Contact: teekayemgee777@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address and name, as they appear on the email you send.
  • Anything you choose to write, such as what worries you about your apps, or whether you'd like to help test.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers see your IP address to show you the page and may keep it in security logs; see GitHub's privacy statement. We don't receive or use it.

What we use it for

  • To email you when the app launches.
  • If you said you'd like to help, to invite you to test the app before launch.
  • To understand, in total, what worries people about their apps, so we build the right thing.

Our lawful basis is your consent, which you give by emailing us to join. You can withdraw it any time by replying to any email from us or writing to the address above.

Who we share it with

Nobody, except Google, which provides our email service (Gmail) and stores messages for us under its own privacy policy, including on servers outside the UK. We never sell your information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you ask to be removed, or 6 months after the app launches, whichever comes first. If the app doesn't go ahead, we'll delete the list.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, or to stop using it. Email teekayemgee777@gmail.com and we'll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first at teekayemgee777@gmail.com. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

This notice covers the waitlist only. The app has its own privacy policy.
