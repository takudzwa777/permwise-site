Permwise privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Applies to the Permwise Android app

The short version: Permwise checks your apps without anything leaving your phone. The app has no internet access, so we never receive your app list, your scan results or your score. There are no ads, analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your information or share it for advertising.

Who we are

Permwise is made by Takudzwa Mugwamba. Contact: teekayemgee777@gmail.com.

What the app does on your phone (we never receive this)

To score your privacy, the app reads:

  • the list of apps installed on your phone, and the permissions and special access each one has (for example accessibility, device admin, keyboard and notification access);
  • the names of the code libraries inside each app you installed, to check for known tracker code;
  • when you last opened each app, only if you allow Usage Access.

The app keeps its results and settings, including apps you mark as safe, in its own storage on your phone. None of this is sent to us or anyone else: the current version of the app doesn't have permission to use the internet at all. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

Usage Access

If you allow it, Usage Access tells Permwise when you last opened each app, so it can point out apps you haven't used in months that still have access to things like your location or microphone. Permwise can't see what you do inside other apps. You can turn Usage Access off at any time in Android settings; the rest of the scan still works.

Tracker check

Permwise looks at the names of the code libraries built into each app you installed and compares them with a list of known trackers that comes with the app. The list is from Exodus Privacy and is used under the Open Database Licence. This happens entirely on your phone: Permwise doesn't open or read your files, messages or anything you've saved in other apps, and nothing is sent anywhere. Finding tracker code means the app contains it, not that it is collecting your data right now.

Your privacy score

The score is worked out on your phone from the permissions and access each app has, any tracker code found, and whether you still use the app. It is a guide to what an app is allowed to do, not a finding that any app misuses your data. No decision with legal or similar effects is made about you.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message to reply. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail). We keep emails for up to 2 years, then delete them. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: answering you.

Children

The app is for a general audience and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of any personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or stop using it. Email teekayemgee777@gmail.com and we'll reply within one month. As the app sends us nothing, the only information we could hold is emails you've sent us.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first at teekayemgee777@gmail.com. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you what we're doing about it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.

Changes

Future versions may add tracker-list updates or paid features. If they change anything above, we'll update this policy before that version is released, change the date at the top, and for important changes tell you in the app.
